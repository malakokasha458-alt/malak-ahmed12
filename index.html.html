I — The Moment

A traffic light that wouldn't turn on

A student I was mentoring in robotics was building a traffic-light circuit. It wasn't working — and he was stuck, quietly frustrated, staring at a board that should have lit up.

I could have just told him what was missing. Instead, I explained the general principle behind the circuit and the code, then kept asking: what do you think is missing? I watched him go from confused to focused, and eventually — he found it himself: a missing resistor, a missing wait in the code.

He wasn't just proud he'd fixed it. He understood, for the first time, the kind of problems he'd run into next. That's the moment I realized: teaching isn't handing someone an answer. It's building their ability to find it.

— this idea now runs through everything I do, from robotics workshops to community volunteering
